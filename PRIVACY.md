# TimeDial Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 3, 2026

TimeDial is a lightweight macOS menu bar app for comparing timezones. Your privacy is important, and TimeDial is built to work fully offline.

## Data Collection

- TimeDial does **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data.
- The app does **not** connect to the internet.
- The app does **not** use analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services.

## Data Stored Locally

TimeDial stores only local preferences on your Mac (for example: selected timezones, compact mode setting). This data stays on your device and is never shared.

## Permissions

TimeDial does not request access to your contacts, calendar, location, microphone, camera, photos, or files.

## Third-Party Services

TimeDial does not integrate with any third-party services.

## Children’s Privacy

TimeDial does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app’s behavior changes, this policy will be updated here with a new “Last updated” date.
